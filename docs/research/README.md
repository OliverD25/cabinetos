# Research notes

This folder holds research notes: studies of other products, and the
proposals drawn from them. A note here is a proposal, not a decision. It
binds nobody until the creator accepts it into [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) or
into a decision record in [decisions/](../decisions/README.md); the
governing documents stay the Constitution, the brief
([ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md)), the plan and the ADRs. The first
note is [total-commander.md](total-commander.md) (2026-09-29): which of
Total Commander's best-loved features and keys CabinetOS has, and a
proposed Phase 11 for the rest.
